# Privacy Policy — LifeMate AI (Project draft)

**Last updated: 9 September 2026**

LifeMate AI is designed as a local-first personal organization app.

## Data stored on the device
Tasks, notes, habits, streaks, app settings and recent assistant chat history are stored locally on the user's Android device. Focus timer state, completed daily focus minutes and recorded habit completion dates are also stored locally.

## Internet use
The core organizer works without internet. If the user chooses to configure the optional AI backend, the current message, recent chat history, up to 30 open tasks, up to 20 habits and excerpts from up to 8 recent notes are sent to that backend and its AI provider so a contextual response can be generated.

## API keys
OpenAI API keys are not stored in the Android application. The optional backend is designed to keep provider credentials on the server.

## Notifications
The app may request notification permission to show task reminders created by the user.

## Accounts and advertising
The app does not require an account and does not include advertising or third-party advertising trackers.
